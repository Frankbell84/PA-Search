Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026.

The PA Lookup Tool is designed to minimize collection of personal information. The current tool does not require an account, name, email address, or login.

Lookup history and dark-mode preferences are stored locally in your browser using localStorage. This information remains on your device unless you clear your browser data or use the tool's Clear History feature.

The tool connects to public or third-party data sources to map ZIP codes to counties and sends users to official government property-appraisal resources. Those external websites have their own privacy practices.

If advertising or analytics services are added in the future, this policy will be updated to describe the technologies used, including cookies or similar identifiers where applicable.

This site is an independent utility and is not a government agency.
